Questions

Will this get my text past AI detectors?

No tool can promise that, and Plainhand doesn't try to. It removes the writing habits people notice and edits toward your own voice. If a school, client or platform asks you to disclose AI use or forbids it, those rules still apply to text you ran through Plainhand.

Does it change my facts?

The rewrite rules forbid adding or dropping facts, names, numbers, dates, quotes and citations, and code, paths and URLs are kept verbatim. Read the result before you use it, the same as with any editor.

Is my text stored?

Scan, Humanize, image-to-text and the free AI rewrite run entirely on your device. A Claude rewrite sends the text to Anthropic's API (directly with your key, or through our relay with Pro). The relay keeps only your monthly usage count.

What is the score?

A count of AI-writing patterns, weighted and scaled to 0 to 100. Under about 50 words it is noise, and the tool says so.

Scanner: sloptrim detect.py (Apache-2.0). Rewrite rules adapted from the humanizer skill (MIT), based on Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing. OCR by Tesseract.js.
